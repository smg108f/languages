Mexican Spanish is the biggest child in the family, having outgrown its parent's headcount decades ago while still sending postcards addressing Spain as the mother country. It softens nearly every word with a diminutive whether or not the thing described is actually small, treats chili as both a food group and a personality trait, and has exported enough telenovelas and ranchera heartbreak that much of the Spanish-speaking world now argues in a Mexican accent by default.

#### eras
---
Mexican Spanish changes with whoever's ruling from Tenochtitlan or Mexico City and what the ruling party calls itself: an imperial Nahuatl-inflected colony, an independent republic, a revolution that rewrote the social contract, a one-party modernization project, and now a NAFTA-era economy broadcasting telenovelas and norteño ballads worldwide.
