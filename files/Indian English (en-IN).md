Indian English is the diligent middle manager of the English-speaking world, having inherited its parent's paperwork, decided the grammar could be more efficient, and quietly taken over the back office. It invented "prepone" out of sheer administrative necessity, answers most of the planet's customer service calls, and views both London and Washington as slightly disorganized head offices it must occasionally humor.

#### eras
---
Indian English changes in step with who's administering the subcontinent and why they need English spoken: first a trading company, then an empire's schoolmasters, then a nation building its own bureaucracy, then a call-center economy answering the rest of the world's phones.
