Canadian English is the sibling who moved out but still comes home for Thanksgiving twice, apologizes to inanimate objects it walks into, and has spent two hundred years insisting it sounds nothing like the neighbours to the south while pronouncing "about" in a way only the neighbours to the south can hear. It ends sentences in "eh" as a built-in consensus-check, calls the last letter of the alphabet "zed" out of politeness to the Crown, and considers a toque, a double-double, and a functioning healthcare system non-negotiable cultural exports.
