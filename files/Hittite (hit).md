Hittite is the language of a king named Anitta who conquered a Bronze Age city, cursed it, and swore no one would ever resettle the site, only for a rival king named Hattusili to ignore that curse a century later and rebuild the ruins into the capital of an empire around 1650 BCE. Its earliest surviving inscriptions, credited to Anitta himself around 1700 BCE, are now recognized as the oldest written record of any Indo-European language on Earth, predating even Mycenaean Greek by centuries. Its army's most audacious stunt came under a later king named Mursili I, who marched roughly 2,000 kilometers south, sacked Babylon itself in 1595 BCE, toppled the dynasty founded by Hammurabi, and then, apparently satisfied, handed the whole city over to some allies and went home. Its charioteers owed their edge to a 184-day conditioning regimen for war horses, written down by a Mitanni trainer named Kikkuli and preserved on clay tablets that modern equestrians still recognize as genuine interval training. Its legal and ritual texts categorized people by exactly what they couldn't do, one surviving line flatly distinguishing "these are blind" from "these others are deaf, these first ones cannot see, these others cannot hear," a level of bureaucratic bluntness that still reads as almost clinical three and a half thousand years later. Its empire came apart during the general collapse that took down most of the Late Bronze Age Mediterranean around 1200 BCE, and its capital sat forgotten and unexcavated until the early 20th century, when archaeologists finally hauled roughly 30,000 clay tablets out of the ground and started teaching graduate students how to read them.

#### eras
---
Hittite changes with whoever's ruling Hattusa: a Pre-Hittite/Anitta Era before Hattusa becomes a Hittite capital, an Old Hittite Kingdom Era once Hattusili I founds the kingdom there, a Middle Hittite Kingdom Era of consolidation, a Hittite Empire/New Kingdom Era once the state reaches its imperial peak, and a Bronze Age Collapse Era once the empire disintegrates.
