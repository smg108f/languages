German is the language that took a good idea — words — and decided the solution to any missing concept was simply gluing more words onto it until a single noun could describe the specific melancholy of watching your own soup get cold. It sorts every noun into one of three genders with no reliable logic, buries the verb at the end of the sentence like a punchline you have to wait for, capitalizes nouns as a matter of grammatical law, and has spent the last two centuries producing an outsized share of the world's philosophy, engineering manuals, and traffic regulations, often in the same tone of voice.

#### eras
---
German changes with whoever's holding the imperial crown and how forcefully the state is organizing itself: a loose patchwork of prince-bishoprics under a resurrected Roman title, a Reformation that gave ordinary German speakers a Bible in their own tongue, a hastily unified empire, a reunified federal republic, and now a global media era exporting car names and compound nouns to the rest of the world.
