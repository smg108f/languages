Philippine English is the world's customer service department, having inherited American English's vocabulary from a boatload of schoolteachers and turned it into the planet's most patient telephone voice. It answers your billing dispute at 3am with a smile you can somehow hear, switches fluidly into Tagalog mid-sentence when the American parent company isn't listening, and has quietly become the reason "for your reference" appears in more emails than anyone intended.

#### eras
---
Philippine English changes with each colonial handshake and the industry that followed it: three centuries of Spanish friars, four decades of American schoolteachers, a democratic restoration, and now a globalized workforce answering the world's phones and typing the world's chat support.
