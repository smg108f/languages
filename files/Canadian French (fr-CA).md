Canadian French is the child that Metropolitan French keeps introducing at parties with a faint wince, having spent four centuries perfecting an accent, a slang, and a set of curse words that no longer resemble the original in any way the parent finds acceptable. It swears using Catholic liturgical furniture instead of anatomy, having turned "tabernacle" and "chalice" into the linguistic equivalent of a clenched fist, insists on saying "magasiner" instead of "faire du shopping" as an act of quiet legislative defiance, and has spent sixty years passing laws to make sure it stays exactly this way, surrounded on all sides by 350 million English speakers who barely notice the fight.

#### eras
---
Quebec French changes with whoever holds Quebec City and how forcefully the province insists on staying French next door to 350 million anglophones: a colonial outpost of Nouvelle-France, a conquered colony under British rule, a confederation partner learning to legislate its own survival, a Quiet Revolution that modernized and secularized almost overnight, and now a linguistically fortified province exporting its own slang back to France.
