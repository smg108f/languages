Tunisian Arabic is the language of a small Mediterranean country that has been conquered by nearly every empire that ever floated a fleet and kept a souvenir from each of them, folding Punic remnants, Berber grammar, Ottoman Turkish, and colonial French into a single "everyday tongue" it calls, without much ceremony, just Derja. It exports more chili paste than any other nation on Earth while insisting that paste is a UNESCO-recognized cultural treasure rather than a condiment, kept an Islamic teaching mosque running continuously since before Charlemagne, produced the first sociologist in recorded history almost by accident, and set off the entire Arab Spring by being the one country where the uprising actually worked.

#### eras
---
Tunisian Arabic changes with whoever's controlling the central Mediterranean coast: a Punic-Berber substrate that reportedly outlasted its own written use by centuries, an Islamic conquest that founded one of the Maghreb's holiest cities within decades of Muhammad's death, an Ottoman-then-French colonial run that layered Turkish and French vocabulary onto the dialect, a 1956 independence under Bourguiba that made Tunisia the Arab world's most secular republic, and a 2010-2011 revolution that toppled a dictator, launched the Arab Spring, and remains the movement's only clear success story.
