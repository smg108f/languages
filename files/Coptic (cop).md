Coptic is the final chapter of the Egyptian language, the same tongue that produced hieroglyphs, hieratic, and demotic, now written in a Greek-derived alphabet with a handful of Demotic signs bolted on for sounds Greek couldn't handle. According to church tradition, Egyptian Christianity itself begins with Saint Mark the Evangelist founding the Patriarchate of Alexandria in 42 CE, launching an unbroken line of patriarchs and turning Alexandria into Coptic Christianity's enduring capital. The written language followed close behind: Old Coptic ostraca from around 200 CE pair traditional Egyptian hieratic words with their new Coptic transcriptions, capturing a language mid-transition into its final alphabet. Coptic Christians paid dearly for their faith under Roman rule, and the persecution under Emperor Diocletian beginning in 284 CE was so severe that Copts simply set their calendar's clock to it, numbering their years from that date as the Era of Martyrs, a dating system the Coptic Orthodox Church still uses today. Coptic monasticism, meanwhile, gave the language and its faith a durable wordless ritual: Saint Anthony the Great, widely regarded as the father of Christian monasticism, is described in Athanasius's Life of Anthony, composed around 360 CE, repelling demonic temptation in the Egyptian desert by making the sign of the cross, a gesture Coptic Christians still trace from forehead to chest to left shoulder to right shoulder in every liturgy. Coptic itself gradually receded from daily speech after the Arab conquest of Egypt in the 7th century, surviving mainly as a liturgical language, until it found an unexpected second life: in 1822, Jean-François Champollion used his fluency in Coptic, the closest living relative of the ancient Egyptian of the pharaohs, to finally crack the hieroglyphic code of the Rosetta Stone.

#### eras
---
Coptic shifts with whoever's running Alexandria or reading it centuries later: an Alexandrian Patriarchate Founding Era once Saint Mark establishes the church, an Old Coptic Written Era once the transitional script is attested, an Era of Martyrs once Diocletian's persecution becomes so severe the calendar itself is reset to it, a Monastic/Ritual Era once the sign-of-the-cross gesture is documented, and a Modern Decipherment Era once Champollion uses Coptic to crack hieroglyphs.
