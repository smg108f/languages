Nigerian English is the loud, confident younger sibling who took the parent's hand-me-down vocabulary, set it to Afrobeats, and now exports it back to the world through Nollywood and streaming charts. It renamed traffic jams "go-slow," invented "next tomorrow" out of sheer disregard for redundancy, and considers any grammar rule negotiable if the sentence sounds better without it.

#### eras
---
Nigerian English changes with whoever is drawing the borders and whoever is holding the microphone: first colonial administrators, then a single amalgamated protectorate, then an independent republic finding its own civilian voice, and now a home-video industry and Afrobeats scene exporting the language's swagger worldwide.
