European Portuguese is the retired sea captain of the family, having mapped and colonized more of the world by 1500 than most empires manage in a lifetime, then spent the last few centuries quietly nursing a glass of port and a very specific kind of melancholy it refuses to translate. It kept the "harder," more consonant-clipped pronunciation its much larger Brazilian child abandoned, insists _saudade_ cannot possibly be rendered in any other language, and now accounts for a small fraction of the world's Portuguese speakers while still behaving, not unreasonably, like the original.

#### eras
---
Portuguese changes with the reach of the empire and the shape of the government back home: a frontier kingdom carved out of the Reconquista, a maritime empire that briefly touched four continents, a republic, a decades-long dictatorship, and now a democracy still working out its relationship with a much larger former colony that speaks the same language differently.
