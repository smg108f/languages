Castellano is the imperial parent who spent five centuries insisting the sun never set on its empire, then spent the next century arguing amongst its own regions about who really speaks it correctly. It distinguishes "c" from "s" with a consonant the rest of the Spanish-speaking world swears is a lisp and Madrid swears is just correct pronunciation, serves dinner at an hour most of Europe considers a light snack before bed, and gave twenty countries a language while reserving for itself the right to be mildly annoyed when they change it.

#### eras
---
Castellano changes with whoever's reconquering, uniting, or democratizing the peninsula: a Latin frontier dialect hardened in the wars against Al-Andalus, an imperial language spread across an ocean, a dictatorship's enforced standard, and now a constitutional democracy sharing the stage with Catalan, Basque, and Galician.
