Gothic is the language whose entire surviving literature exists mostly because a fourth-century bishop needed an alphabet to translate scripture into it, cobbling one together from Greek, Latin, and runic letters around the same time he's recorded being consecrated bishop of the Gothic Christians in 341 CE. Its speakers split into two branches after a Hunnic invasion shattered their unified territory in the 370s, one branch storming into Rome itself under Alaric in 410 CE, the other conquering Italy under Theodoric the Great, who took Ravenna in 493 CE by inviting his rival to a reconciliation banquet and personally cutting him down with a sword ten days later. Its assemblies, like those of the wider Germanic-speaking world that the Roman historian Tacitus described around 98 CE, registered approval not by ballot but by warriors clashing their spears together, reserving a groan for whatever they didn't like. Its Iberian branch built a kingdom around Toledo that outlasted the Italian one by over a century and a half, until a single defeat in 711 CE ended it outright, and its most isolated dialect kept limping along in Crimea long after every other Gothic community had gone quiet, surviving just long enough for a sixteenth-century Flemish diplomat to record about 80 of its words from two elderly speakers before it too disappeared. Its chief physical legacy is a deluxe manuscript written in silver and gold ink on purple-dyed parchment, looted and re-looted across four different countries before landing in a Swedish university library, where most of its visitors can admire it without being able to read a word.

#### eras
---
Gothic changes with whoever's migrating or ruling: a Migration Era while Gothic-speakers move from Scandza toward the Vistula and Black Sea, a Christianization/Biblical Era once Wulfila devises an alphabet and translates scripture, a Divided Kingdoms Era once Hunnic pressure splits the Goths in two, a Twin Capitals Era once Toledo and Ravenna anchor separate Gothic kingdoms, and a Remnant Era once the Visigothic kingdom falls and only Crimean Gothic survives.
