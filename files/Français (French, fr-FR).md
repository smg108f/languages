French is the language that appointed itself the official arbiter of taste, diplomacy, and cuisine, then founded an actual government institution whose primary job is telling everyone else, including its own speakers, that they're using it wrong. It maintains nearly four centuries of formal vocabulary rulings against the relentless creep of _le weekend_ and _le marketing_, insists on gendering every noun as though furniture has opinions, and still expects the rest of the world to agree that its accent is the romantic one.

#### eras
---
French changes with whoever's unifying the Franks or rewriting the constitution: a Germanic kingdom's court language grafted onto Vulgar Latin, a monarchy that founded an academy to police its own grammar, a revolution that rewired the state from scratch, a parade of republics and empires, and now a Fifth Republic broadcasting itself in cinema and haute cuisine.
