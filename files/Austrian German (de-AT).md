Austrian German is the branch of the family that still lives in the old imperial house long after the empire itself was sold off, insisting on its own words for potatoes, tomatoes, and January while the German relatives down the road use perfectly serviceable different ones. It calls its bureaucratic titles "Hofrat" with a completely straight face, considers a good cup of coffee a matter of civic identity worth defending to UNESCO, and quietly maintains that its version of German is the more melodious one, an argument it has never once won and never once stopped making.

#### eras
---
Austrian German changes with whoever's ruling from the Hofburg and how large the empire currently is: a duchy claimed by an ambitious dynasty, a sprawling multiethnic empire, a compromise state sharing a crown with Hungary, a fragile first republic squeezed between two world wars, and now a small, neutral, thoroughly rebuilt federal republic still insisting its German is the more melodious one.
