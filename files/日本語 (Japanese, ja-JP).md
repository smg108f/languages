Japanese is the language of an island archipelago so committed to written redundancy that it runs three separate scripts simultaneously, having borrowed Chinese characters wholesale and then invented two additional phonetic alphabets just to explain them properly. It treats a precisely sliced piece of raw fish as a legitimate art form, elevated a wrestling match between two enormous men into a Shinto ritual spanning over a millennium, survived the only wartime use of nuclear weapons in history, and somehow parlayed all of this into exporting animated cartoons that now outgross most of the rest of the planet's cinema combined.

#### eras
---
Japanese changes with whoever's controlling the archipelago's imperial court: an Old Japanese era recorded in borrowed Chinese characters, a classical Heian era that produced the world's first novel and two homegrown phonetic scripts, a medieval era of shogunates and civil war, an Edo era of prolonged isolation and internal peace, a Meiji era that rushed to modernize after centuries of seclusion, and a post-1945 era rebuilt from the only wartime nuclear attacks in history into a global cultural and technological exporter.
