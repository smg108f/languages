Australian English is the exiled cousin who was shipped to the far side of the planet in chains and decided, against all reasonable expectation, to have a great time about it. It shortens every word it can reach out of equal parts affection and heat exhaustion, exports "no worries" as a fully-formed philosophy of life, and treats the world's deadliest wildlife as a minor scheduling inconvenience.
